How it works

  1. You enter student details. Background information plus reading and writing scores.
  2. The data is prepared. Categories are encoded and values scaled the same way as during training.
  3. The model estimates the score. A trained machine learning model returns a predicted math score out of 100.

Predictions are statistical estimates based on past patterns, not a judgment of any individual student.
